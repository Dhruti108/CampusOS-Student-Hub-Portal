General Questions

What is CampusOS?

CampusOS is a student hub portal that provides access to academic information, campus services, announcements and other student resources.

How do I create a CampusOS account?

Go to the Register page, enter your student information and complete the registration process.

How do I log in to CampusOS?

Open the Login page and enter your registered username and password.

Can I access CampusOS from my mobile phone?

Yes. CampusOS can be accessed through a web browser on computers, tablets and mobile devices.

Academic Questions

Where can I check my courses?

You can view your current courses and course details from the Academics section.

Where can I check my marks?

Your subject-wise marks and grades can be viewed through the Academics page.

Where can I check my attendance?

Subject-wise attendance information is available in the Academics section.

Where can I find my examination schedule?

Examination dates, subjects and examination venues are available on the Academics page.
