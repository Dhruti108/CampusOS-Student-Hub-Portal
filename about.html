About CampusOS

CampusOS is a student-focused digital platform created to make college life simpler, more organized, and more connected.

Who We Are

CampusOS is a web-based Student Hub Portal that brings important academic information, campus services, announcements, and student support into one convenient platform. Instead of searching through different sources, students can use CampusOS to find the information they need in one place.

The portal is designed with students in mind. It provides easy access to academic resources, campus facilities, important announcements, frequently asked questions, and support services. Our goal is to create a simple and user-friendly digital campus experience.

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide students with a simple, secure, and efficient platform that helps them manage their academic needs, discover campus services, and stay connected with important college information.

Our Vision

Our vision is to create a smart digital campus where students can access useful academic information, campus services, and student resources anytime and from anywhere.

What CampusOS Provides

📚 Academic Support

Access courses, timetables, examinations, assignments, and other academic information.

🏫 Campus Services

Find information about library, transport, hostel, cafeteria, sports, and other campus facilities.

📢 Important Updates

Stay informed about college announcements, events, deadlines, and student activities.

💬 Student Support

Get answers to common questions and contact support whenever assistance is required.
